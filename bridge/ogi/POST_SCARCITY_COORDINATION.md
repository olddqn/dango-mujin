# Post-Scarcity Coordination

> Abundance is not enough. The hard problem is coordination.

---

## What Changes When Money Loses Central Meaning

Money has always been a coordination technology, not an end in itself.

When automation drives the cost of production toward zero —
when code can be copied without cost, when translation can be generated,
when logistics can be optimized, when much analysis can be automated —
the question is not whether money disappears.

The question is: **what does coordination look like when money no longer sets the terms?**

What becomes central:

| Was central | Becomes central |
|---|---|
| Price | Trust |
| Transaction | Contribution |
| Ownership | Access |
| Wealth accumulation | Coordination capacity |
| Contract enforcement | Dignity agreement |
| Market clearing | Negotiation |
| Capital allocation | Attention and care |
| Scarcity management | Abundance governance |

This does not mean money vanishes.
It means money becomes one signal among many —
and often not the most important one.

---

## The Danger: Abundance Without Negotiation

A common assumption about post-scarcity is that it resolves conflict.

This is wrong.

Abundance without coordination produces a different kind of scarcity:
- **Attention** becomes scarce (infinite content, finite human attention)
- **Trust** becomes scarce (anyone can claim anything, verification is hard)
- **Dignity** becomes scarce (scale makes exploitation easy and cheap)
- **Coordination itself** becomes scarce (everyone can act; few can act together)

The historical record is clear:
- Agricultural abundance produced hierarchy and war, not peace
- Industrial abundance produced inequality, not sufficiency for all
- Information abundance produced manipulation, not truth

Post-scarcity does not self-organize into fairness.
It requires a coordination layer — a protocol for negotiation, contribution, and memory.

## What Dan-Go Provides

Dan-Go's role in a post-scarcity context is not to distribute resources.
It is to provide the **negotiation structure** through which resources are coordinated.

A Dan-Go Claim is not a market bid.
It is a public statement about a desired state:
> "This vacant building can become a shared creative space."
> "This person's story can generate support while preserving their dignity."
> "This community's local knowledge can be compensated fairly."

The claim opens a negotiation — not a transaction.

The negotiation involves:
- Multiple parties with different capabilities and stakes
- Public record of every objection and amendment
- Dignity constraints that cannot be traded away
- Contribution tracking across money and non-money forms
- Reality feedback that reports what actually happened

This is coordination without requiring a price for every interaction.

## The Five Coordination Requirements

For post-scarcity to avoid collapse, coordination requires:

### 1. Memory
Past negotiations must be recorded and queryable.
Without memory, every interaction starts from zero.
Exploitation can repeat without accountability.
Dan-Go: **su-table append-only log**

### 2. Transparency
Coordination cannot happen in secret.
Hidden negotiation is how power concentrates.
Public negotiation is how coordination distributes.
Dan-Go: **公開談合 (public negotiation)**

### 3. Dignity Constraint
The coordination protocol must have a non-negotiable floor.
No optimization can proceed if it violates human dignity.
This is not a preference. It is a hard constraint.
Dan-Go: **Dignity Guard**

### 4. Contribution Recognition
Value must be acknowledged across non-monetary forms.
Care, translation, local knowledge, safety review —
these are not free. They must be credited.
Dan-Go: **Contribution tracking / Su-table**

### 5. Reality Feedback
Intent is not outcome.
Coordination must close the loop between what was planned and what happened.
Dan-Go: **Reality Feedback events**

## Coordination as the Scarce Resource

In a post-scarcity economy:
- computation is cheap
- storage is cheap
- communication is cheap
- generation (of text, code, translation) is cheap

What remains scarce:
- the ability to coordinate multiple parties toward a shared goal
- the trust required to act on each other's contributions
- the dignity protection required to include the most vulnerable
- the memory required to build on past negotiations rather than repeat them

Dan-Go is a protocol for managing coordination scarcity.
Not by rationing coordination — by making it transparent, bounded, and memorable.

## Implications for Agent Design

AI agents operating in a post-scarcity context face a specific challenge:

They can produce abundance (of text, code, analysis, translation)
but they cannot, alone, coordinate the human context in which that abundance is meaningful.

An AI agent that ignores dignity constraints is not a coordinator.
It is an extractor.

An AI agent that follows Dan-Go's protocol:
- Submits Claims to public negotiation
- Records objections and accepts amendments
- Tracks contributions across all forms
- Respects the Dignity Guard as a hard constraint
- Reports reality feedback honestly

...is a participant in post-scarcity coordination, not an obstacle to it.

The OGI compatibility layer in this bridge is designed for exactly this kind of agent.

## Reasoning in Post-Scarcity Coordination

In a post-scarcity context, the coordination bottleneck is not resources — it is
structured reasoning about how to allocate them fairly. Abundance does not
automatically produce coordination; it requires agents that can reason about
conditions, constraints, and consent before acting.

Dan-Go addresses this with the **reasoning surface** — a plan tree layer
that sits between language and execution:

```
Language surface:   "This vacant house should become shared space."
                    ↓
Reasoning surface:  goal → dignity clearance → condition resolution → decision
                    ↓
Execution surface:  contribution / task / su-table event
```

The plan tree makes the reasoning verifiable:
- Did the plan account for all dignity constraints?
- Are all action nodes tied to declared capabilities?
- Does the plan reach a terminal conclusion, or does it abstain?

In post-scarcity coordination, a plan that **correctly abstains** is more
valuable than one that proceeds without checking constraints. Abundance does
not override dignity. The plan tree encodes this as a structural requirement.
