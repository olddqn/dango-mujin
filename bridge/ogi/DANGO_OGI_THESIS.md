# Dan-Go × OGI — Central Thesis

> When money loses central meaning, coordination becomes the scarce resource.
> Dan-Go treats impossible claims as coordination problems.
> OGI-style agent economies may provide the operating environment for that coordination.

---

## The Problem Both Are Solving

Money is a coordination technology.

It allows strangers to cooperate without trust.
It converts heterogeneous contributions into a common unit.
It lets value flow across time and space.

But money also concentrates.
It can be withheld.
It can be used to coerce.
It can exclude the people whose contributions are hardest to price.

When automation drives the marginal cost of most production toward zero,
money's coordination function does not disappear —
but it is no longer sufficient on its own.

A translator's work, a caregiver's attention, a community's local knowledge,
a safety reviewer's judgment — these resist reduction to a price.
Not because they have no value. Because they have *kinds* of value
that markets handle poorly.

OGI imagines an economy that can hold all of these values simultaneously.
Dan-Go provides the negotiation layer for how they are exchanged.

---

## What Dan-Go Provides

Dan-Go is a protocol for turning impossible claims into real ones
through public negotiation.

A claim is a statement about a desired state:
> "A vacant house can become a shared creative base for displaced artists."

It is not a demand.
It is not a transaction.
It is the beginning of a negotiation.

The negotiation involves:
- Objections (what is missing, what is wrong)
- Amendments (proposed modifications)
- Contributions (who can do what)
- Dignity constraints (what cannot be done regardless)
- Reality feedback (what actually happened)

Every step is public.
Every step is append-only.
Every step is queryable.

This is what coordination looks like when money is not the organizing principle:
not a market, but a **public negotiation with memory**.

---

## What OGI Provides

OGI (Open General Intelligence / Open General Economy — exact implementation status unverified)
imagines an environment where:

- AI agents and humans cooperate on tasks that neither can do alone
- Contribution is tracked across many dimensions, not just monetary ones
- Trust and reputation are coordination signals, not just wealth
- Streams of value flow continuously between agents rather than in discrete transactions
- The economy is designed for abundance, not scarcity

In an OGI-style economy:
- A Dan-Go Claim becomes an **agent task** — a structured request for coordination
- A Contribution becomes a **credit signal** — evidence of value created
- The Dignity Guard becomes the **exploitation boundary** — a constraint the economy cannot cross
- The Su-table becomes **shared memory** — the history that coordination relies on

---

## The Synthesis

When you combine Dan-Go's negotiation structure with OGI's agent-economic context:

**Dan-Go Claim** = What the economy should do next, stated publicly  
**OGI Agent Task** = The structured assignment that emerges from that claim  
**Dan-Go Contribution** = The work that moves the task forward  
**OGI Credit Signal** = The economy's acknowledgment of that work  
**Dan-Go Dignity Guard** = The rule that the economy cannot cross  
**OGI Exploitation Boundary** = The same rule, in agent-economic terms  
**Dan-Go Reality Feedback** = What actually happened in the world  
**OGI Outcome Record** = The economy's memory of that outcome  

The combination produces a coordination protocol that is:
- **Public** — no secret negotiation
- **Bounded** — dignity cannot be violated
- **Memory-bearing** — nothing is forgotten
- **Agent-compatible** — AI and human participants use the same interface
- **Non-monetary** — money can participate, but cannot dominate

---

## What This Bridge Does Not Claim

This bridge does not claim that:
- OGI exists in a working form
- Post-scarcity has arrived
- Money is obsolete
- AI agents are equivalent to human participants
- This protocol will solve inequality

It claims only:
- If an OGI-style economy emerges, Dan-Go can provide its negotiation layer
- The conceptual mapping between Dan-Go primitives and OGI concepts is coherent
- The bridge can be extended to a real OGI implementation when one is available
- The dignity constraint survives the translation intact

---

## The Core Principle

> Abundance without coordination becomes collapse.

A post-scarcity economy that does not coordinate
produces not utopia but chaos:
- resources flow to whoever can extract them first
- contributions go unacknowledged
- dignity is violated at scale
- the most vulnerable remain excluded

Dan-Go is the negotiation structure that prevents abundance from becoming collapse.
The su-table is the memory that prevents coordination from being forgotten.
The dignity guard is the boundary that prevents optimization from becoming exploitation.

Together, they are the governance layer for an economy that has outgrown money
but has not yet learned to coordinate without it.
