# Recognition Remains External — Dan-Go Protocol Note

> **"Recognition remains external."**
> **"Appeal is not enforcement."**

## What "Recognition Remains External" Means

In the Dan-Go protocol, **recognition** — the decision to credit a contribution
economically — is always made by an external system. Dan-Go does not make
this decision. Dan-Go does not influence this decision. Dan-Go observes the
outcome of this decision.

"Recognition remains external" is a permanent architectural principle, not
a temporary limitation to be overcome in a future phase.

## The Full Chain

```
Contribution occurs
    ↓ (Dan-Go records)
Candidate credit classified (Phase 11)
    ↓ (Dan-Go observes)
External credit observed/not observed (Phase 12)
    ↓ (Dan-Go reflects)
Unrecognized contribution recorded (Phase 13)
    ↓ (Dan-Go records appeal)
Advisory appeal filed (Phase 14)
    ↓ (external system decides — Dan-Go does not)
Recognition decision (external)
```

At every step, Dan-Go records. At no step does Dan-Go decide recognition.
The decision remains external throughout the entire chain.

## Why External Systems Are Sovereign

External credit systems (GITSEA, others) are sovereign because:

1. **They have the relevant authority**: GITSEA controls its own treasury
   and stream eligibility. Dan-Go has no access to these.

2. **They have the relevant context**: External systems may apply criteria
   Dan-Go does not track — governance votes, stake thresholds, time windows,
   on-chain conditions.

3. **They are independent actors**: External systems have their own
   governance, users, and objectives. Dan-Go cannot substitute for their
   judgment without becoming a competing authority.

4. **Sovereignty is by design**: If Dan-Go could override external systems,
   it would no longer be a neutral negotiation protocol. It would be a
   governance system. That is not what Dan-Go is.

## What Dan-Go Can Do

Dan-Go can:
- Make contribution legible (Phases 8-11)
- Make the gap between contribution and credit visible (Phase 12)
- Record that the gap was observed (Phase 13)
- Record that reconsideration was requested (Phase 14)
- Keep all of this permanently observable and append-only

Dan-Go cannot:
- Issue credit
- Compel external systems to respond
- Override external credit decisions
- Grant or revoke recognition
- Determine whether contribution deserves credit

## What "Remains External" Implies About Future Phases

In any future phase of the Dan-Go protocol, recognition will still remain
external. Future phases may:
- Improve legibility of contribution records
- Add new observation mechanisms
- Record new types of appeals or signals

Future phases will not:
- Give Dan-Go authority to issue credit
- Make Dan-Go's classifications binding on external systems
- Change the `authority: none` invariant

"Recognition remains external" is a terminal principle. It does not expire.

## Protocol Phrase

> "Recognition remains external."

This phrase appears in every Phase 14 runtime module. It is a statement
of architectural design — not a concession or apology. Dan-Go's value
is in making contribution observable and legible. The economic outcome
of that legibility is, by design, determined elsewhere.
