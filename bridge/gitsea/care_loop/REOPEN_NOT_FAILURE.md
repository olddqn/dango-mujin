# Reopen Is Not Failure — Dan-Go Protocol Note

> **"Reopen is not failure."**
> **"Follow-up is not blame."**
> **"Care loop is not obligation."**

## The Distinction

**Reopening** a care case is an advisory observation that the care situation
has continued, changed, or surfaced new needs after the initial relief case
was recorded. It is an act of paying attention — of noticing that care is
ongoing rather than finished.

**Failure** is a negative judgment about what happened before. A reopen
does not judge the original response. The `reopen_is_failure: false` and
`reopen_certifies_failure: false` invariants on every reopen record make
this explicit and unconditional.

## Why Recurring Need Is Not Failure

Care needs recur. Food insecurity does not resolve with one meal. Housing
insecurity does not resolve with one advocacy contact. Displacement does not
resolve with one supply delivery or five nights of shelter. This is the
nature of the situations that Jammy House, D.R.A., and similar commons
respond to.

When Dan-Go records a reopen because a need has recurred, it is recording
a fact about the situation, not a verdict on the response. The food that
was provided was real. The shelter that was offered was real. These things
happened and are permanently recorded in the Phase 18 care memory.
The reopen adds new information: the situation continued. It does not
subtract the value of what was done.

## Why Partial Outcomes Are Not Failures

An outcome of 'partial' in Phase 18 means assistance was observed to have
addressed part of the request. It does not mean the response was
inadequate. In housing advocacy, initiating a negotiation is a partial
outcome — the tenancy question is not resolved yet. In refugee relief,
supplying a household addresses immediate material need — it does not
resolve displacement. These are partial outcomes in the sense that more
may follow. They are not partial failures.

When Phase 19 records a reopen following a partial outcome, the reopen
reason captures what remains to be observed — not what went wrong.

## What Reopen Records Accomplish (Without Judgment)

A reopen record:

1. **Extends the observable care history.** The community can see that
   the care situation did not end with the Phase 18 record. History is
   continuous. The loop continues.

2. **Makes recurring need legible without stigmatising it.** A person
   who needs food support twice is not failing to solve their food
   insecurity. They are in a situation that requires ongoing attention.
   The reopen record makes that visible without attaching shame.

3. **Enables voluntary coordination of follow-up.** By recording what
   follow-up is needed, the Phase 19 layer gives participants in the
   commons the information they need to choose whether to respond again.
   The choice remains theirs.

4. **Preserves the append-only care record.** The original Phase 18
   records are not modified. The reopen appends to them. History
   grows with the situation.

## Protocol Phrase

> "Reopen is not failure."

This phrase appears in every Phase 19 runtime module. It is the boundary
between observation and judgment — the commitment that Dan-Go's record of
a care case being reopened will never constitute a verdict on the original
response, the original participants, or the original situation.
