# Forecast Is Not Certainty — Dan-Go Protocol Note

> **"Forecast is not certainty."**
> **"Preparedness is not command."**
> **"Hint is not allocation."**

## The Distinction

**A forecast memory** — in the Dan-Go sense — is a preparedness record
derived from an observed recurring pattern. It points forward from past
observations in the same way a weather observation might point forward:
it says this has happened before, and it may happen again. It is not a
guarantee. It is not a prediction with a confidence interval. It is not
an expert determination.

**Certainty** is a claim that something will happen. Dan-Go makes no
such claim. The `forecast_is_certainty: false` invariant on every Phase 21
record is unconditional and permanent. A forecast memory is a structured
observation about the past. Its forward-pointing quality is in its form,
not in any epistemic authority it holds.

## Why the Distinction Matters in Care Contexts

The communities that Dan-Go records — Jammy House, D.R.A., YaCypherpunks
— operate in conditions of genuine uncertainty. Food insecurity is not
a mechanical process that repeats on a schedule. Displacement does not
resolve or recur according to a predictable pattern. Tenancy disputes
depend on negotiations that have not yet happened.

If forecast memories were treated as certainties:

1. **Resources might be committed in advance of expressed need.** A commons
   told with certainty that food need will recur might build permanent
   meal infrastructure that strains its resources, only to find that the
   situation changed. Certainty leads to over-commitment.

2. **People might be anticipated rather than heard.** If a person known to
   have needed food support before is assumed to need it again, their agency
   in expressing or not expressing new need is bypassed. Certainty replaces
   listening with assumption.

3. **The forecast would create expectations.** If a commons is told with
   certainty that displacement relief will be needed, participants who do
   not provide relief may feel they failed to meet an expected standard.
   Certainty creates obligation by the back door.

Dan-Go avoids all of these by making the forecast explicitly uncertain.
The confidence label on every forecast record names how weak the
grounding is. "Observed pattern only" is the strongest label available —
and it means only that something happened before.

## What the Forecast Memory Accomplishes (Without Claiming Certainty)

1. **Extends care history forward in a legible form.** Participants reading
   the forecast memory know that a type of need has occurred before and
   may find it useful to hold that in mind. The forward-pointing form helps
   with voluntary planning without requiring any commitment.

2. **Supports voluntary community reflection.** A commons may choose to
   reflect on its recurring care patterns — to understand what its members
   and neighbors have needed, to notice what responses have worked, to
   consider whether any voluntary coordination makes sense. The forecast
   memory supports this reflection. It does not initiate it.

3. **Preserves contestability.** A participant who believes the forecast
   memory is misleading — that the confidence label is too high, that the
   pattern is coincidental, that the situation has changed — can contest
   the record. The forecast memory is advisory and contestable. It is not
   an authoritative projection.

## The Confidence Label System

Every forecast record carries a confidence label:

- `single_observation` — One instance. This is the weakest possible signal.
  It may not recur. It may have been a unique circumstance.
- `two_observations` — Two instances. An emerging pattern, but also possibly
  coincidence. Very low confidence.
- `observed_pattern_only` — Multiple observations; no causal analysis.
  This is a pattern of past events, not a forecast mechanism.
- `four_plus_observations` — The most observations in the current Phase 21
  dataset. Still not certainty. The pattern is stronger, but displacement,
  food insecurity, and tenancy disputes are not mechanical systems.

No label implies a probability. No label implies a recommendation. The
label describes what was observed. The community interprets what it means.

## Protocol Phrase

> "Forecast is not certainty."

This phrase appears in every Phase 21 runtime module. It is the boundary
between Dan-Go's preparedness memory and any claim about what will happen.
What has happened is legible. What will happen is the community's to discover.
