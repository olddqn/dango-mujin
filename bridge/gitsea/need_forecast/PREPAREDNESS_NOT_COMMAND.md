# Preparedness Is Not Command — Dan-Go Protocol Note

> **"Preparedness is not command."**
> **"Forecast is not certainty."**
> **"Hint is not allocation."**

## The Distinction

**Preparedness** — in the Dan-Go sense — is voluntary awareness of what
might be useful given observed patterns of care need. A preparedness hint
says: this type of capacity awareness may be useful to this commons given
what has been observed. It points at information that the community might
consider. It points at nothing the community must do.

**Command** is an instruction backed by authority. An instruction that
creates obligation, that can be enforced, or that produces social
consequence for non-compliance is a command. Dan-Go has no authority.
Dan-Go cannot enforce anything. Dan-Go cannot produce social consequence.
The `preparedness_is_command: false` and `hint_compels_action: false`
invariants on every Phase 21 record are permanent.

## The Full Chain of Voluntariness

The care layer has refused coercive interpretation at every phase:

- Phase 17: Need is not debt. Help is not command. A person in need does
  not owe reciprocity. A person offering help is not commanded to give.
- Phase 18: Relief is not proof. Outcome is not judgment. Recording a care
  outcome does not certify or judge the response.
- Phase 19: Care loop is not obligation. Reopening a care case does not
  compel anyone to respond again.
- Phase 20: Learning is not prescription. Pattern memory does not instruct.
- Phase 21: Preparedness is not command. A hint about capacity awareness
  does not require any participant to acquire, hold, or deploy capacity.

Each phase has the same refusal: the record is not a directive.

## Why Preparedness Hints Cannot Become Commands

If preparedness hints were understood as commands:

1. **Participants would feel obligated to prepare for anticipated need.**
   A commons that reads a hint about meal capacity awareness might feel
   that it is expected to build that capacity before need is expressed.
   This obligation would exist before any new request was made — before
   any new person asked for help. This is the opposite of voluntary care.

2. **Failure to prepare would become a kind of negligence.** If the hint
   commands preparation and the commons does not prepare and a need arises,
   the commons would be seen as having failed to act on a warning. This
   attaches blame to inaction — exactly the dynamic that Phase 17 through
   Phase 20 refused to allow.

3. **Voluntary cooperation would become structured obligation.** The care
   layer is built on the principle that cooperation is chosen, not required.
   Commands cannot coexist with voluntary cooperation. A preparedness hint
   that becomes a command destroys the voluntary character of the system
   it is part of.

Dan-Go avoids this by building the limit into every record. The hint is
information. The community uses information as it chooses. Information
that is not chosen does not cease to exist — it simply was not acted upon.
This is not failure.

## What Preparedness Hints Accomplish (Without Commanding)

1. **Surface useful information for voluntary planning.** A commons that
   wants to think about its recurring care patterns — that wants to discuss
   whether to coordinate recurring meal support or sustain advocacy
   relationships — now has structured information to bring to that
   discussion. The hint reduces the effort of surfacing information. It
   does not reduce the community's authority over what to do with it.

2. **Make preparedness legible without requiring it.** A community that
   reads the hint and concludes it is not in a position to build capacity
   has read the hint and made a decision. The decision is valid. The record
   of the hint does not mark that community as unprepared or negligent.
   The record simply says: this information was available.

3. **Support contestability.** If a community believes the preparedness
   hint is inappropriate — that the pattern is coincidental, that the
   suggested capacity awareness is not relevant, that the hint mischaracterizes
   the situation — it can contest the record. The hint is advisory and
   contestable. It is not a standard to be met.

## The Limit of Preparedness Memory

Dan-Go records preparedness hints. Dan-Go does not:
- Set a preparedness target for any commons
- Measure whether a commons met a preparedness standard
- Report non-preparation as a failure
- Alert any participant that preparation is required
- Decide what level of capacity is sufficient

The preparedness memory ends at the boundary of the record. Everything
beyond that boundary is the community's to determine.

## Protocol Phrase

> "Preparedness is not command."

This phrase appears in every Phase 21 runtime module. It is the commitment
that Dan-Go's preparedness hint will never become a directive. The hint
is for the community's awareness. What the community does with that
awareness is the community's to decide.
