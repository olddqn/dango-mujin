# Resource is not allocation.

Dan-Go / GITSEA Bridge · Phase 51 · authority: none · advisory only

---

## The Principle

> **Resource is not allocation.**

Recording that a commons *has* a resource is not a record that the
resource *has been assigned* to anyone, any need, or any purpose. A
resource may sit in a record forever without ever being allocated, and
that record remains valid.

---

## Why This Matters

If a resource record were read as an allocation:

- Recording would become assignment by default
- Resources would be drafted into roles before their holders consented
- The act of *making the resource visible* would itself transfer it
- Holders would learn to hide what they have, to remain free to use it
- Visibility would become an extraction mechanism

The whole purpose of a resource record — making mutual presence legible —
collapses the moment it is mistaken for an allocation.

---

## The Distinction

| Concept | What it is | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Resource (Phase 51) | observed possession | holder |
| Allocation | "this resource → this need" | nobody decides this in Dan-Go |
| Transfer | actual change of possession | only the holder, voluntarily, in real life |

Dan-Go records the first. It does not perform, suggest, recommend, or
record the second or third.

Even when a `resource_memory` record places a resource and a need side
by side, Dan-Go does **not** join them. Juxtaposition is for human
reading; assignment requires the holder's own voice.

---

## The Invariant

```yaml
resource_is_allocation: false
memory_matches_resource_to_need: false
memory_transfers_ownership: false
```

These fields are present on every record produced by Phase 51 — registry,
snapshot, memory, and report. They are permanent and never flip.

---

## In Practice

A commons reviewing its own resource record should be able to say,
without contradiction:

> "Yes, we have this. No, we are not giving it to anyone right now."

Both halves are true at once. Existence is the first half. Allocation —
if it ever comes — is a separate, voluntary, human act that Dan-Go
does not record as automatic and never infers from a registry entry.

---

> Resource is not allocation.
> Possession is not obligation.
> Availability is not transfer.
