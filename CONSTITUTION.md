# Dan-Go Mujin Constitution

**Do not violate the dignity of another.**

That is the only law.

Everything else is protocol.

---

## Articles

**Article 1**
Impossible only means not enough negotiation yet.

**Article 2**
Lies are unrealized state transitions.

**Article 3**
Anyone may propose a Claim.

**Article 4**
Claims must separate observed state, required state, and desired state.

**Article 5**
Negotiation must be publicly auditable whenever possible.

**Article 6**
Violence, coercion, and exploitation are forbidden means of realization.

**Article 7**
Reputation comes from contribution, not status.

**Article 8**
Dan-Go itself may be modified through public negotiation.

**Article 9**
Forks, objections, and counterclaims are valid participation.

**Article 10**
Do not violate the dignity of another.

---

## Notes on Interpretation

- Article 1 does not mean everything is possible. It means the question of possibility is always open to negotiation.
- Article 2 does not excuse dishonesty. It reframes falsehood as a gap to be closed, not a person to be blamed.
- Article 10 is both the last article and the foundation of all others. When articles conflict, Article 10 takes precedence.

---

*This constitution may be amended through public negotiation (see MUJIN_PROTOCOL.md). Any amendment must itself satisfy Article 10.*
